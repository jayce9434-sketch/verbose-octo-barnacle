BUGBREAK: COLONY SIEGE — UPDATE 1: NIGHTFALL & HAUNT
=====================================================

This is a flat, GitHub-Pages-ready continuation of the solo v6 rebuild. Multiplayer remains completely removed.

UPDATE 1 CONTENT
- 4 modes: Colony Siege, Nightfall Hard Mode, Halloween Haunt, Harvest Rush.
- Hard Mode: permanent night, stronger Nightmare enemies, denser waves, redesigned enemy presentation, and separate hard-mode boss music.
- 11 playable bugs total. Firefly is the new Update 1 bug. Ghost Moth is the Halloween completion reward.
- 20 regular enemy species, including new Dobsonfly and Mole Cricket.
- Boss1, Boss2, Boss3, Boss4 and Boss5 class enemies appear during non-Halloween runs.
- Normal campaign: 50 waves and 10 Apex bosses.
- Halloween Haunt: 20 waves, 3 haunted biomes, 2 themed bosses + Hollow Queen final boss, Candy Corn currency, 4 haunted upgrades, and Ghost Moth unlock for beating Wave 20.
- Halloween upgrade ranks and Candy are automatically removed after the Halloween event ends.
- Harvest Rush: 25 waves, autumn biomes, leaf-storm presentation, Harvest Stag final boss, and a completion Gem bonus.
- 13 permanent Gem upgrades, including the new Boss Breaker upgrade.
- 28 run mutations and 14 boss relics.
- Permanent upgrades visibly change the player's insect: armor bands, crown-fangs, speed fins, crit eyes, boss crest, lifesteal core and seasonal effects.
- Mutations and relics visibly evolve the insect during a run. Relics orbit the body and several mutation families add armor, mandibles, energy trails, auras or electric arcs.
- Evolution HUD shows the most recent active mutations/relics.
- 36 original music tracks in this build: 5 normal tracks, 2 Nightfall ambience tracks, 3 Halloween ambience tracks, 2 Harvest ambience tracks, 10 normal boss themes, 10 Nightfall boss themes, 2 Halloween boss themes, 1 Halloween final theme, 1 Harvest boss theme.
- 14 generated SFX for combat, alerts, Candy, wave starts, haunting, Nightfall and victory.
- Updated app icon and visual pass.

Ant, Termite, Ladybug, Fly, Mosquito, Dragonfly, Bee, Stinkbug, Spider, Firefly, Ghost Moth.
Ghost Moth stays locked until Halloween Haunt is beaten.

CONTROLS
WASD / Arrow Keys = Move
Space = Bite
Shift = Dash
E = Special ability
R = Repair nest while nearby
Escape = Pause
Touch/iPad = joystick + Bite / Dash / Special / Repair

GITHUB PAGES
1. Create a GitHub repository.
2. Upload every file from the ZIP directly into the repository root.
3. There are no folders in the ZIP.
4. Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from branch -> main -> /(root).
5. Open the Pages link.
